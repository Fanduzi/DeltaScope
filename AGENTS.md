# Agent Guidance

Read and follow the linked guidance when its trigger applies:

- **Every task, including non-coding work:** consult [decision-record requirements](CODING_STANDARDS.md#decision-records-for-every-task) and [final-report requirements](CODING_STANDARDS.md#before-every-final-report).
- **Before exploring:** follow [domain guidance](docs/agents/domain.md) for this single-context repository's glossary and decision-record conventions.
- **When fetching, creating, or updating requests:** follow [issue-tracker guidance](docs/agents/issue-tracker.md).
- **When applying triage roles:** use [triage-label mappings](docs/agents/triage-labels.md).
- **Before code or documentation changes, including docs-only work:** follow [change requirements](CODING_STANDARDS.md#before-code-or-documentation-changes) and [verification requirements](CODING_STANDARDS.md#during-development-and-verification).
- **Before commits or pull requests:** follow [commit and PR requirements](CODING_STANDARDS.md#before-commits-or-pull-requests).
- **When planning or executing a milestone:** follow [milestone workflow](CODING_STANDARDS.md#when-planning-or-executing-a-milestone).
- **Before pushing, tagging, or releasing:** follow [authorization requirements](CODING_STANDARDS.md#before-pushing-tagging-or-releasing).
- **When writing a release handoff:** follow [handoff requirements](CODING_STANDARDS.md#when-writing-a-release-handoff).
