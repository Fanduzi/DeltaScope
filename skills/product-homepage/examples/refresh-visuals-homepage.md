# Example: Keep Narrative, Refresh Visuals

## User request
"Keep the current homepage story, but make the UI and layout feel fresh."

## Expected behavior
- preserve the homepage story skeleton
- preserve product boundaries and trust evidence
- change at least three visual dimensions from the canonical list, such as typography system, color and contrast system, background treatment, hero composition, card and panel system, or illustration or icon style
- call `frontend-design`
- explain what changed visually and what stayed stable structurally
